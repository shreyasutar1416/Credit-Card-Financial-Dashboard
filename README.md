# 💳 Credit Card Financial Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Credit Card Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze credit card transactions and customer behavior. The dashboard provides key business insights into revenue generation, transaction volume, customer demographics, spending patterns, and card usage trends.

This project consists of two analytical dashboards:

1. **Credit Card Transaction Report**
2. **Credit Card Customer Report**

Together, these dashboards help stakeholders understand customer spending habits, revenue drivers, card category performance, and demographic trends for better decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall credit card revenue and transaction performance.
- Analyze customer demographics and behavior.
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Understand spending patterns across categories.
- Compare performance across card categories and payment methods.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## 📈 Business Insights

- Blue Card customers contribute the highest share of revenue.
- Swipe transactions generate the largest revenue compared to Chip and Online methods.
- Graduate customers form the largest revenue-generating education segment.
- Business professionals and White-collar customers contribute significantly to total revenue.
- Revenue distribution varies across income groups, age groups, and states.
